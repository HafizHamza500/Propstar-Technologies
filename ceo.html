CEO MESSAGE

At PROPSTAR TECHNOLOGIES, our journey began with just a few believers and a handful of cold calls. What we lacked in numbers, we made up for with unwavering determination, resilience, and a shared vision to build something impactful. Over time, we didn’t just earn the trust of our clients—we cultivated a culture that attracted passionate, talented individuals who shared our mission. That trust has become the foundation of our growth, reflected today in both the strength of our team and the diverse range of services we proudly offer.

From the very beginning, PROPSTAR TECHNOLOGIES was never just about delivering services—it was about building synergies, solving real problems, and guiding our clients toward scalable, sustainable success. Whether it’s off-market land acquisition, contract modeling, or BPO services, we’ve always focused on providing agile, tailored solutions that create lasting value.

When I first stepped into the business world, I didn’t have all the answers—and I didn’t wait for the perfect conditions. What I did have was the drive to act, adapt, and figure things out along the way. Every challenge became an opportunity to sharpen how I think, lead, and grow. That mindset has shaped PROPSTAR TECHNOLOGIES into what it is today.

"The only limits are the ones you set for yourself. Push past them. Then push past them again."

We don’t just work for our clients—we work with them. We empower our partners with the tools, insights, and technology they need to make informed decisions and stay ahead in a rapidly changing landscape. Our purpose is simple: to drive impact, create value, and continuously evolve in ways that benefit our clients, our people, and the communities we serve.

We don’t just react to opportunities—we create them.

Looking ahead, I’m excited about what the future holds. If you’re ready to break past your limits and redefine what’s possible, we’re ready to build with you.

Shawn Surani
Founder and CEO, Propstar Technologies

Innovating the future, empowering talent to create exceptional digital experiences.
