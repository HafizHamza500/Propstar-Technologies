Frequently Asked Question

I’m a fresher. Will I still get promoted if I perform well?

Yes, Many of our team leads and trainers began as freshers. If you perform, learn quickly, and stay consistent, growth will follow—no experience barriers here.

Will I receive training before starting?

Absolutely. You’ll undergo a 3-day training program covering call flow, tone, objection handling, and real-time practice—so by Day 3, you’re confident and floor-ready.

How do I apply?

Just bring your CV and a valid ID. No extra documents or prior experience required—come as you are; we’ll handle the rest.

What’s the work environment like?

Expect a professional yet friendly culture where learning, collaboration, and growth happen side by side

What does career growth look like at PropStar?

We offer clear career paths with regular performance reviews, training opportunities, and leadership mentoring for those who want to move up.

Do I need prior experience?

No. We welcome freshers and beginners — our training equips you with everything you need to start strong
